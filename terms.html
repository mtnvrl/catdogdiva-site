Free tries

Without a subscription you can try the app for free: a few sound plays, a few AI translations and one AI outfit photo per iPhone. Free tries are limited and may change.

Cat & Dog Pro

AI translations and the full sound library are part of Cat & Dog Pro, offered as:

  • Cat & Dog Pro Weekly — an auto-renewing subscription billed every week.
  • Cat & Dog Pro Yearly — an auto-renewing subscription billed every year.

Prices are shown in the app before you buy. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. A subscription renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. AI translations have a weekly fair-use limit that resets every week. It is generous for normal use and keeps the service fast and affordable for everyone.

Diva Points

  • Diva Points are a one-time, non-subscription purchase used for AI outfit photos: 1 Diva Point = 1 outfit photo. No subscription is needed.
  • Diva Points never expire. They have no cash value, can't be transferred or exchanged, and are used only in the app.
  • A point is used when an outfit photo is made. If a photo can't be made because of a problem on our side, the point is returned. If the AI can't use your photo (for example, it doesn't show a cat or dog clearly), the point is returned a few times a day; after that, the point is used.
  • A finished outfit photo waits on our server until the app collects it, for at most 3 days. If it isn't collected in that time (for example, because the app was deleted), the photo is deleted and the Diva Point it used is returned. A photo made with a free try isn't given back.
  • Refunds for purchases are handled by Apple under its policies. If Apple refunds a Diva Points purchase, those points are removed from your balance, even if some of them were already used (your balance can go below zero).
