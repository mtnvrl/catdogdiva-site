Cat & Dog Diva Support

How does translating work?

On the Translate screen, choose cat or dog, then a direction. Cat → Me: record your pet and get a playful AI reply. Me → Cat: say something short to your pet, and the app plays the real cat or dog sound closest to your mood. It's just for fun, not a real translation.

“We couldn't hear your pet / you clearly”

The app checks a recording before sending it. Hold the phone close, keep it quiet around you, and record your pet in Cat → Me or your own voice in Me → Cat. A recording that can't be used doesn't count against your translations.

How do outfit photos and Diva Points work?

Open Outfits, pick a look, then take or choose a clear photo of your pet. Each outfit photo uses 1 Diva Point. Your first photo on a new iPhone is free. Diva Points never expire and don't need a subscription. Finished photos are kept in My Gallery; tap Save to add them to your photo library.

My photo wasn't accepted

Use a bright, sharp photo where one cat or dog and its face are clearly visible. A cat photo works with cat outfits and a dog photo with dog outfits.

I bought Diva Points but don't see them

Points usually appear within seconds. Make sure you're online, then pull down on the Settings screen to refresh. If they still don't appear, email us with the date of your purchase.

Free tries

Without a subscription you get a few sound plays, a few AI translations and one AI outfit photo per iPhone.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with. Diva Points stay with the app on your iPhone, even if you reinstall it.

Cancel a subscription

On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Cat & Dog Pro.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete my data

In the app, go to Settings › Delete My Data. You can also email us. Your Diva Points balance is deleted too and can't be restored. See the Privacy Policy for what is deleted and what is kept.
