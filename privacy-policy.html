CatDog Diva Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what Cat & Dog Translator Diva (“CatDog Diva”, “the app”, “we”) does with your information. In short: there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Recordings and pet photos are sent to an AI service only when you use an AI feature, and we don't keep them.

No accounts, no tracking

CatDog Diva has no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email, contacts or location, we don't use advertising or analytics tools, we don't track you across other apps and websites, and we never sell information.

Anonymous app ID

The app creates a random ID the first time it opens and keeps it in your iCloud Keychain, so your Diva Points and subscription follow you if you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone. This ID is not linked to your name or Apple Account. It is sent with every request to our server and to our purchase service (RevenueCat).

AI features (OpenAI)

The AI features use OpenAI's API. Before the first AI feature, the app asks for your permission; you can turn AI processing off anytime in the app's Settings.

  • Translations: when you record your pet (up to 10 seconds), or record yourself talking to your pet (up to 6 seconds), the recording is sent through our server to OpenAI, which writes a playful reply or picks a pet sound. Other sounds in the room may be in the recording. Before sending, the app checks on your iPhone that the recording contains a pet or a voice, so empty recordings never leave your device.
  • Outfit photos: the pet photo you choose or take is sent through our server to OpenAI to create the outfit picture. Before sending, the app checks on your iPhone that the photo shows a cat or dog.

We don't store your recordings or pet photos. OpenAI processes them under its API terms and doesn't use API data to train its models; it may keep API data for up to 30 days to monitor abuse, then deletes it (openai.com/policies).

Our server

Our server runs on Supabase in the European Union (Ireland). It stores only what the app needs to work:

  • your anonymous app ID, your Diva Points balance and its history (purchases, photos made, refunds);
  • usage counts for the weekly fair-use limit and the free tries;
  • the cost of each AI request (no content), so we can keep prices fair;
  • a finished outfit picture, only until the app collects it, and at most one day;
  • to stop abuse, your IP address may be used to count requests; these counters are deleted within a day.

Free tries (Apple DeviceCheck)

Free AI tries are given once per iPhone. To check this, the app uses Apple's DeviceCheck service, which lets us store two yes/no flags for your device with Apple. DeviceCheck doesn't tell us who you are or give us any device identifier.

Purchases

Apple handles payment through the App Store, and we never see your payment details. To manage CatDog Pro and Diva Points, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. RevenueCat receives your anonymous app ID, your purchase history (product, price, dates, renewals, refunds) and basic technical data (device model, iOS version, app version, country and language, IP address). It is used only to unlock Pro, credit Diva Points, restore purchases and handle refunds, under RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Refund requests

If you ask Apple for a refund of an in-app purchase, Apple may ask us how the purchase was used. With your agreement under our Terms of Use, our purchase service (RevenueCat) answers on our behalf with usage information only: how much of the purchase was used (for example, Diva Points spent), how long the app has been used, and your purchase and refund totals in the app. No recordings, photos or other content are shared. Apple uses this only to decide on the refund.

On your iPhone

Your outfit photos (My Gallery) are stored in the app on your iPhone. Saving to your photo library happens only when you tap Save, and the app asks for permission to add photos. The camera and microphone are used only when you take a photo or record. Deleting the app deletes My Gallery.

Your choices and rights

You can turn AI processing off in Settings, and manage or cancel a subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. You may ask us what data we hold about your anonymous ID, or ask us to delete it (this also deletes your Diva Points balance): email us with the date of a purchase or of your last use so we can find the record. If you live in the EU, UK or Türkiye, you have rights under the GDPR / KVKK, including access, correction and deletion, and you can contact your data protection authority.

Children

CatDog Diva is a family-friendly app but is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Children under 13 should use the AI features only with a parent or guardian.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date above.
